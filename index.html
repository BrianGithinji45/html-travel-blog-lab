Adventures in Fire Island

My recent trip to Fire Island National Seashore was an unforgettable adventure. I spent my days exploring the pristine Atlantic beaches, hiking through maritime forests, and climbing the historic Fire Island Lighthouse for breathtaking views. The park's natural beauty, wildlife, and peaceful trails made it a perfect getaway from the city

On the second day, I visited the historic Fire Island Lighthouse. I climbed 182 steps to the top and saw a beautiful view of the island and the ocean. The guide told us stories about how the lighthouse helped sailors for over 100 years.

On my last day, I explored the Sunken Forest and nature trails. The maritime forest felt magical with its twisted trees and quiet paths. It was the perfect end to my Fire Island adventure.
